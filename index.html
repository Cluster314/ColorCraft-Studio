Color Physics Guide

1. Additive Physics (RGB Light) [DEFAULT]

Models combining light spectrums. Red + Green strictly produces bright Yellow! Adding another drop of Red (Red + Yellow) shifts the balance to Vivid Orange. Red + Green + Blue = Pure White.

2. Subtractive Physics (RYB / Paints)

Models physical paints where pigments absorb light. Mixing Red + Green produces Earthy Olive/Brown. Yellow requires Yellow pigment or Blue + Yellow for Green.
